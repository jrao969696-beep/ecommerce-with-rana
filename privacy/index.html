Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Ecommerce with Rana Limited · Company No. 17597018

This policy explains how Ecommerce with Rana Limited collects, uses and protects personal information. Please read it carefully.

Who we are

Ecommerce with Rana Limited is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. Our registered office is Office 4059xp, 182-184 High Street North, East Ham, London, E6 2JA. You can contact us at ecommercewithranaa@gmail.com or on +44 7888 676470.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address and telephone number; your company details; identification and address documents you provide for company formation, account opening or referral services; your inquiry subject, message and service of interest . We also receive limited technical and security information needed to deliver the website, prevent spam and keep the service secure.

How and why we use it

To respond to inquiries, prepare quotations and provide the services you ask us for, including steps you request before entering into a contract.

To refer you, with your agreement, to our partners (for example, business banking and company registration providers such as Tide) and to pass them the information they need to process your application.

To operate, protect and improve the website and our business, where this is necessary for our legitimate interests and does not override your rights.

To meet legal, regulatory, accounting or fraud-prevention obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Who receives your information

We share information only where needed: with partners you ask us to refer you to; with service providers that help us run the website and answer inquiries, including Cloudflare for hosting and security and our business email provider; and with professional advisers or public authorities where required by law.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we rely on an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual and organisational safeguards required by UK data-protection law.

How long we keep it

Website inquiries are sent to our business mailbox and are not intentionally stored in a separate website database. We normally keep correspondence for up to 24 months after the last contact, unless it becomes part of an ongoing client relationship or a longer period is required for legal, accounting or dispute purposes.

Cookies, analytics and security

The website uses functionality needed for security, navigation and forms. Optional analytics must not be enabled until visitors are given appropriate information and, where required, a consent choice. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, this notice and the website controls will be updated.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or an objection to certain uses. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. These rights can be limited by law. Contact ecommercewithranaa@gmail.com to make a request.

Complaints and updates

Please contact us first if you have a privacy concern. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We may update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change; the date above shows the latest revision.
